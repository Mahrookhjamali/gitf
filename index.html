A LITTLE NOTE FROM MY HEART

Just For You ♡

A reminder to keep dreaming and smiling.

Hey Gentleman! ♡

You know what? I really hope ki aap apne saare dreams achieve karein. Not just the big ones, but even those little goals jo shayad aapne abhi tak kisi ko bataye bhi nahi hain.

Mujhe nahi pata aapka future exactly kaisa hoga, but I genuinely wish ki woh aapki expectations se bhi zyada beautiful ho.

Bas ek baat hamesha yaad rakhiyega, apne career aur dreams ko lekar kabhi khud par doubt mat kijiye. Har successful person ki journey mein difficult days aate hain. Kabhi opportunities late milti hain, kabhi plans work nahi karte, aur kabhi khud par bhi doubt hone lagta hai. But that doesn't mean you're not capable.

Aap bas seekhte rahiye, grow karte rahiye aur apni mehnat jaari rakhiye. Kisi aur ki speed se apni journey compare mat kijiye. Aapka time bhi aayega, In Sha Allah. Aur jab aaye na, toh apni success ko enjoy karna mat bhooliyega… chahe us waqt main aapke saath rahun ya nahi.

Aur suniyega, sirf successful hi nahi, happy bhi rehna hai. Career important hai, lekin aapka sukoon aur health bhi utne hi important hain. So please, apna khayal rakha kijiye. Thoda rest, thodi masti aur woh cute si smile bhi maintain rakhiye. Itna serious hokar duniya nahi jeetni. :')

And there's something I want you to know...

Aapki life mein chahe kitne bhi naye chapters aayein, kahin ek jagah koi hai jo aapke liye genuinely bahut achha chahti hai.

Koi hai jo Allah se dua karti hai ki aapko woh opportunities milein jinke aap deserve karte hain. Aapki mehnat kabhi waste na ho. Aapke liye career ke doors open hon, aapke decisions mein khair ho aur aapko har us jagah success mile jahan aap apna best de rahe hain.

Aur jab kabhi aapka din achha na ho, tab bhi aapki khairiyat ki dua karne wala koi hai. Jab aap kisi goal ke kareeb pahunchein, toh aapki khushi ke liye khush hone wala bhi koi hai.

Maybe aapko har baar iska ehsaas na ho, but your happiness genuinely matters to someone.

My little wish for you:

Ek din aap apni favourite life jee rahe honge. Aapke paas aapki favourite car hogi, jiske passenger seat par aapki Hoor-e-Pareshan aapke saath baithkar long drive par jaayegi. Aap kisi Big 4 company mein honge, apne career mein woh position achieve kar chuke honge jiske liye aaj mehnat kar rahe hain, aur jab peeche mudkar dekhenge, toh apni poori journey par proud feel karenge.

May Allah bless your efforts, protect your dreams, make your difficult paths easier and give you success that comes with peace. Ameen. 🤍

So, Gentleman, keep going. Don't give up on yourself. Aap mein bahut potential hai, bas khud par bharosa banaye rakhiye.

And yes, jab bhi koi achhi news mile na, toh smile zaroor karna. Kuch log hain jo aapki good news sunkar aapse bhi zyada khush honge. ♡

Always making duas for your happiness and success,

Your Hoor-e-Pareshan. 🤎
G
Keep smiling, Gentleman. ♡
